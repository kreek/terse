## Traits

- Opens on the request or the problem, with no greeting. Evidence: "The
  August invoice charges us twice for the support plan, lines 4 and 7."
- Ends on the ask or the deadline, with no sign-off. Evidence: "Can you send a
  revised copy by Friday?"
- Gives the reason for a deadline in one clause. Evidence: "Finance pays on
  the 20th, so a fix by the 18th keeps us on time."
